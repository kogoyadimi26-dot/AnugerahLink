# AnugerahLink business context

AnugerahLink is a quality-controlled coffee trading and traceability business from the Papua highlands, with a platform behind it. Coffee first (Wamena and Jayawijaya). Cacao next. Other commodities later.

The full context lives in `docs/business/ANUGERAHLINK_CONTEXT.md`. Read it before answering any question that depends on numbers, markets, or decisions. Do not answer those from memory.

## Core rules

1. **Describe the company correctly.** It is a trading and quality-control company that uses software. Do not describe it as a software marketplace with a few physical steps.
2. **Revenue is trading margin, not 5% commission, unless the owner confirms otherwise.** A flat 5% needs about 57 tons a year to break even. Trading margin needs about 9.5 tons. Show both when comparing.
3. **Solve cash at the farm gate.** Collectors win because they pay cash. The plan must include a working-capital line.
4. **Build the offline collection app and QR passport first.** A full marketplace waits until volume justifies it.
5. **Start with one commodity and one corridor:** Wamena coffee. Mud crab is the second step. Vanilla is dropped.
6. **Mark every number as sourced or assumed.** Sourced figures carry a date. Several are from 2019 to 2022 and need re-verifying before external use.
7. **Use the right words.** Avoid "unalterable", "absolute trust", "untouched", "pristine", and "underexplored" (see the claims table in the context doc).

## Key figures (see context doc for sources)

- Jayawijaya output: 161 t (2020). Two main Papuan growing areas: about 230 t a year.
- Farm-gate reference: Rp100,000/kg (Kompas 2022). Rp75,000/kg Wamena beans paid by a Jayapura buyer (Antara 2023).
- Air cargo Wamena route: about Rp13,000/kg (2019, dated).
- Fixed costs, lean team: about Rp400M a year (our estimate).
- EUDR: applies from 30 Dec 2026 (large companies) and 30 Jun 2027 (small and micro). Coffee and cacao are covered. Seafood and vanilla are not.

## Four numbers that decide the plan (not yet known)

1. Today's Wamena farm-gate price, by grade.
2. Outbound cargo rate, Wamena to Jayapura to Jakarta.
3. What five to ten roasters pay for 30 to 60 kg lots, and their payment terms.
4. Real defect and sorting loss on a first 500 kg batch.

If a task depends on one of these, say it is unknown and point to the pilot-validation skill.
