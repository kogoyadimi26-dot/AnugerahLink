# Digital opportunities in Papua, Timika and Tembagapura

Research date: 19 November 2026. Ranked by evidence of need, speed to first revenue, and capital required. Figures come from news and official press releases and are indicative. Verify before committing money.

## Recommendation

Start with #1. It is the only option that builds directly on the logistics thesis and generates revenue from the first order. Its core is the ordering, stock by hub and credit ledger built here.

Then test #2 in Timika, because the mine is the largest formal buyer in the region. Build the vendor registry and a contractor request board. Charge a listing fee or a lead fee.

Defer #3 to #6 until #1 and #2 show repeat orders.

## Timika and Tembagapura notes

- Freeport reports about 27,000 to 30,000 workers, of whom roughly 40% are Papuan (OAP). Local hiring is concentrated in Mimika Timur, Kuala Kencana and Tembagapura.
- Production is still recovering from the September 2025 Grasberg block-cave mudslide. Output was about 67% of forecast capacity in 2026, with full capacity targeted for 2027 or 2028. Contractor demand is therefore volatile.
- Two employees were killed in shootings in February and March 2026, so the security risk for staff and deliveries in the mining area is real.
- Mimika inflation is generally modest, but food prices move with distribution costs.
- Gaps: no data on household employment or service vendors inside Tembagapura. A follow-up search on contractor layoffs and local service vendors is needed.
